## Hi there 👋

Welcome to my profile.

I'm a first-year master's student conducting research in machine learning, with a particular focus on abstraction. My work explores how machines can move beyond surface-level recognition to achieve a more conceptual and structural understanding of objects. I am interested in the mechanisms that allow systems to form, manipulate, and utilize abstract representations, bridging the gap between raw sensory data and higher-level reasoning.

## Research Interests

- **Abstraction in Machine Learning:**  
  Investigating computational methods that enable AI to generalize and form concepts, rather than relying on mere pattern matching. My current focus is on how abstract representations can be acquired and leveraged for more robust and adaptive learning.

- **Object Understanding:**  
  Exploring models and algorithms that allow machines to infer the essence of objects—how to represent, distinguish, and reason about entities beyond their observable features.

- **Cognitive and Conceptual Modeling:**  
  Drawing inspiration from cognitive science to inform the development of machine learning systems capable of flexible and context-sensitive understanding.
