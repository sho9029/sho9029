# Hi, I’m sho9029.

I’m a master’s student studying machine learning, and I’m interested in how we form abstractions from experience. If the first dogs you meet are all small, size might seem like part of what makes a dog a dog. As you meet dogs of different sizes and shapes, you get a better sense of what they share and how much they can vary. I’m curious about how that broader understanding takes shape, one experience at a time.

I approach that question through incremental learning. I study how machine learning models can build on what they’ve already learned as new examples arrive. I’m especially interested in how abstractions form and change over the course of learning.

I also build small tools for everyday tasks. I’ve made a Windows tray app for checking a mouse’s battery level at a glance and a video encoder that checks the quality of the compressed video. I mostly use Python for research and video processing, and C# for Windows apps.
